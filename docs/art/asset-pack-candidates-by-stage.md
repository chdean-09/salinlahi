# Side-scenery packs, by stage

Written 2026-09-07. Supersedes the single list in `asset-pack-candidates.md` for
stage-specific choices; that file still holds the general notes on grid and licence.

The three settings, taken from the art that defines them:

| Stage | Setting | Palette to match |
|---|---|---|
| **Ugat** | Jungle village behind the map01 palisade | Warm earth (`#875618` family), foliage ramp `#253f1c` → `#cac74f` |
| **Ugnayan** | Sandstone town behind the map02 gate (East-Asian roof, cyan scroll shrine) | Sandstone `#5d4946` → `#d5cdb3`, blue-grey `#343861` → `#b4c6dc`, cyan glow |
| **Pamana** | Slate town behind the map03 arched gate (carved-wood, gold-trimmed American-era shrine) | Slate `#0f1220` → `#84a0b6`, wood and gold `#4a2316` → `#e5b662` |

Requirements unchanged: 3/4 perspective, licence stated with commercial and credit terms,
fit to the 32 px grid. 16 px packs need a 2× nearest-neighbour upscale (lossless, reads
chunkier). Licence terms are what the pages state or the publisher's standing terms;
**read the licence on the page before shipping**, and where a term was not visible it
says so. Ranked best-fit first within each stage.

## Recommendation

Two sources, not six. **LPC for Ugat and Pamana** ([LPC] Jungle + OPP Jungle for the
village; Colonial + Victorian + Castle for the slate town) gives one internally
consistent 32 px style across two stages with the era-correct buildings, at the price of
credits and share-alike on the art. **Ninja Adventure for Ugnayan** is CC0 and already
East-Asian; it is 16 px, so the 2× upscale is the visible cost, and its brighter palette
should be pulled toward the sandstone ramp. Cainos Basic supplies rocks, fences and roots
at the lane edge on all three without clashing.

Still nothing Filipino: bahay kubo, nipa and bamboo palisades do not exist as a pack. The
walls and shrines you already have will keep carrying the era identity, and commissioned
props remain the long-term answer for the margins.
