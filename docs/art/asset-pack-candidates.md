# Side-scenery asset packs — candidates

Written 2026-09-07 for the margin strips beside the lane. Brief: 3/4 (top-down JRPG)
perspective with trees, buildings and rock faces that show a front face; free, with the
licence stated; compatible with the 32 px grid and the saturated, keylined, hue-shifted
palette of the shipped sprites.

Licence terms below are what the pack pages state as of today, or are the publisher's
standing terms (Kenney, LPC, OpenGameArt item licences). **Read the licence text on the
page before shipping.** itch.io "free for commercial use" is a per-author licence, not a
standard one. Where a term was not visible, it says so.

Grid: 32 px packs drop in. 16 px packs need a 2× nearest-neighbour upscale, which is
lossless but reads chunkier than our own tiles (half the detail density). Anything
authored at 48 px or larger needs a lossy downscale and is flagged.

## Not a fit

- **Game-Icons.net** (https://game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0): monochrome symbol icons, not scenery. Useful for UI glyphs, nothing for the margins.
- **OpenGameArt collections** such as *Top Down 2D JRPG 32x32 Art Collection* (https://opengameart.org/content/top-down-2d-jrpg-32x32-art-collection) and *Top-Down Assets*: browsing lists, not packs. Every item carries its own licence; OGA can generate a credits file per collection but does not guarantee it.

## What none of them give you

Nothing free is Filipino. Bahay kubo, nipa, bamboo palisades and Baybayin-era stonework
do not exist as a pack; the nearest are OPP's jungle (tropical, generic), Ninja Adventure
(bamboo and huts, but Japanese), and Tropical Shores (paid, generic). For the two town
stages the era identity will keep coming from the walls and shrines you already have.
Commissioned or hand-drawn props for the margins remain the long-term answer; any pack
above is a milestone-build substitute.

## How they slot in

The margin strips are baked from `StageBackgroundSO.marginLeft/marginRight` (64×256 px,
tiling in Y). A pack's trees and buildings can be composed into such strips by hand in
Aseprite, or sown as props through `render_props` in
`scripts/art/generate_stage_backgrounds.py` — the placement system is the part worth
keeping from the generated attempt. Either way the scene wiring does not change.
